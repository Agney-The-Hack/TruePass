PRIVACY-FIRST BIOMETRIC VERIFICATION MODEL
TEMPORARY EPHEMERAL IMAGING • ZERO ON-CHAIN BIOMETRICS

True Pass follows a privacy-first biometric verification model. Unlike traditional systems that may retain facial images, our approach uses facial verification only to authenticate the ticket holder at the event gate.

Once verification is completed, the temporary facial image is deleted. We combine this with Quai blockchain technology to maintain transparent ticket ownership and secure resale transactions without putting sensitive biometric information on-chain.

Our goal is simple: verify the attendee, validate the ticket, and minimise the storage of personal biometric data.
